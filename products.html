Things People Ask Us

Can I run more than one ad product at the same time?
Yes. If you want to run multiple ad products together, our Platinum package is the best fit because it includes all of our products in one package. Silver and Gold are better suited for advertisers who want to focus on a smaller mix of placements. If you need a different combination, we can also build a custom package around your goals.
How quickly can my campaign go live?
Once we have everything we need from you, we can typically get your campaign live within 1 business day.
How do I know which package is right for my budget?
We offer Silver, Gold, and Platinum packages designed for different goals and budgets. Silver is a great entry point, Gold adds more visibility, and Platinum gives you the most complete package. If you're not sure which one is right, reach out and we'll help you choose the best fit.
Can I change or upgrade my package later?
Yes. You can start with the package that fits your current goals and upgrade when you're ready for more visibility.
Do I need to provide the content or creative, or do you help with that?
You provide the content, creative, and general direction, and we take care of the rest. Our packages include creative support with one round of revisions, so we can help get everything ready for your campaign.
Can I target just my town or a specific area?
Yes. Our packages are available by Daily Voice local site, so you can choose the town or area that matters most to your business. This helps you stay focused on the readers you actually want to reach.
Can I target areas beyond my town?
Absolutely. You can add additional towns or Daily Voice local sites if your business serves more than one community. Each additional town is available at 50% off, making it easy to expand your reach while keeping your campaign local.
How do I get started?
The easiest way to get started is to choose the package that best fits your goals and fill out our contact form. Someone from our team will get back to you shortly. You can also email us directly at advertisers@dailyvoice.com.
